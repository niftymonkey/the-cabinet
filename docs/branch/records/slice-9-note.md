# Slice 9: the grave grows smoothly, and its blinking border is gone

## What changed

**The rules.**

- `src/game/grave.ts`. `Grave` gains `owed`, the growth the grave has been paid and has not yet taken in, and `createGrave` sets it to 0. `growGrave` keeps its name, its signature and its overflow and stops writing the size: it measures the room against the size plus what is already owed, pays what fits into `owed`, and hands back the rest. A new private `takeInOwedGrowth` is the swell: it takes `Math.min(owed, swellPerSecond / TICK_HZ)`, moves it from the debt to the size, clears the score rung when the size has reached `SCORE_RUNG_REARM_SIZE`, runs `containGrave`, and returns one `grew` event carrying what it took in. `ageGrave` becomes `(grave, swellPerSecond) => SimEvent[]` and reads as the invulnerability counting down and then the swell. `hitGrave` takes `HIT_SHRINK` out of the grave's true size, its size plus its debt, and the visible size gives as much of it as it can at once. `TICK_HZ` is imported from `./clock`.
- `src/game/step.ts`. One call site: `events.push(...ageGrave(state.grave, state.conditions.tuning.growth.swellPerSecond))`, still last in the tick.

## Decisions made

- **The swell computes the size as the true size less the debt, rather than adding a slice to it each tick.** A running sum drifts: forty slices of 0.075 off the floor land at 20.999999999999971 rather than 21, and the score rung the size has to reach is a hard comparison that a hair under never satisfies, so growing a full hit's worth off the floor would silently stop giving the rung back. The form in the code is exact at every figure I checked, including the 40-tick and 61-tick cases and a 20,000-tick run of random payments. Only add, subtract and min, so it is the same on every engine. To reverse: sum the slices and give the rung rule a tolerance instead.
- **The new invariant is one identity and not two, and it is recoverable.** One identity because `checkSize` is the precedent for a check with two ends, and `record` keeps one fault per identity per tick either way. Recoverable because the debt itself poisons nothing downstream: a negative debt makes the swell take in nothing, so the grave stops growing, and a debt that would carry the grave past the ceiling is caught fatally one tick later by `size within floor and ceiling`, which is the guard that owns health. To reverse: move it to `fatal` in `FAULT_SEVERITY`.
- **`payGrowth` keeps its name and loses its `events` parameter.** The entry says it keeps its body, and it cannot: the entry also rules that `swallow.ts` stops pushing `grew`, which was the only thing the parameter was for. It stays a named step rather than being inlined because every other line in `swallow` takes the state and `growGrave` takes the grave. To reverse: inline it.
- **The code comments cite "Mark's ruling of 2026-09-21" rather than "design record R-next".** The design record has no R-next section and I did not add one, because the entry does not ask for a design record edit and a design position is Mark's to land. A citation that points nowhere is worse than a dated one. To reverse: the ruling gets its number in the design record and the comments take it.
- **Test 20's name says "the share of the whole climb it was ruled at, nine corpses of eighty" rather than "a ninth".** The entry's own arithmetic is 9 of 80, which is 11.25% and not a ninth, and 45 of 400 is the same 11.25%. The test asserts the share. To reverse: nothing to reverse, the figure is the entry's.
- **`RESERVOIR_IN_CORPSES` is exported.** The entry writes it as a local const. It is exported so the relation test can say the capacity is the corpses its own row names rather than pinning the magnitude 300, which is what `tuning.test.ts`'s own header forbids. Its only reader today is that test. To reverse: unexport it and let the test pin the figure.

## Landing: the fault's severity (fix agent)

CodeRabbit raised `growth owed in range` as wrongly recoverable. I traced every reader of `owed` (`growGrave`, `takeInOwedGrowth` and `hitGrave` in `grave.ts`, `checkRunNoNaN` and `checkOwedGrowth` in `invariants.ts`, `foldGrave` in `witness.ts`, and `payGrowth` in `swallow.ts`) and the finding is right, though not for the reason it gives. The decisive reader is `hitGrave`, which takes `grave.size + grave.owed` as the grave's true size and compares that against `SIZE_FLOOR` to decide whether the floor ladder runs. A probe run against the working tree, a grave at `SIZE_START` 27 with `owed` set to -10, produced `graveHit,scoreBled` and took the score from 5000 to 3000: the ladder ran on a grave standing nine units clear of the floor, and with no score left to bleed the same path strips a rung off every line and then sets `state.ending = 'sealed'`. That is the run's own tally and its ending, both decided off the broken number. The same probe showed a second reader: `growGrave` measures its room as the ceiling less the size and the debt, so the broken grave's overflow on a 100-unit payment was 49.5 against the honest grave's 59.5, and `swallow.ts` pays overflow straight into `state.score`.

Both halves of the note's original reason turned out false. A negative debt does not merely stop the grave growing: the probe showed a payment of 5 units carries `owed` from -1 to 4 and the grave grows again, so the debt is not even self-announcing. And a debt above the ceiling is not caught "one tick later" by `size within floor and ceiling`: from the floor with 100 owed, the probe measured 660 ticks, eleven seconds, before the size passed the ceiling and the fatal check could see it.

Changed, all in the worktree and uncommitted: `apps/hungry-grave/src/game/faults.ts` (`growth owed in range` is `fatal`; the `FAULT_SEVERITY` JSDoc's recoverable paragraph for it is replaced by a fatal one saying the debt is health in transit and naming both readers; the fatal count moves from five checks and six identities to six and seven, the recoverable count from nineteen to eighteen; and the `FAULT_IDENTITIES` header, which this slice left saying twenty-four identities against twenty-five checks, now says twenty-five against twenty-six). `apps/hungry-grave/src/game/__tests__/faults.test.ts` (the closed-list test is renamed to "seven of them fatal" and its expected array gains the identity, plus a new test, "counts the growth the grave is owed as health rather than as bookkeeping"). `apps/hungry-grave/src/game/__tests__/invariants.test.ts` (two comments in the owed-growth block carried the two refuted claims above and now state what the trace found). `src/game/invariants.ts` and `src/tape/wireCodes.ts` are untouched: neither states a severity, and the identity keeps wire code 25.

Green after the one-word change.

No pin moved.
