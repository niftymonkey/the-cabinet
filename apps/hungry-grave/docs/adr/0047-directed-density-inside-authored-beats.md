# Directed density inside authored beats

The stage keeps its authored skeleton, phases, boss boundaries, and the named template library, but density and timing inside a phase become directed: the game reads the pressure the run is putting on the player and shapes the space between beats, scaling frequency rather than amplitude, over the authored rows as a floor.

The signal is the pressure the player is under, not the power the player holds (ruled by Mark 2026-09-01). Left 4 Dead, the lineage this borrows from, reads how hard the player is being pressed; it has no power progression, so it never faced this choice. The one game that scales to accumulated power is Battle Garegga, whose rank rose with power and fell on death, which is the suicide loop this project's standing caution names: a director that reads performance invites Battle Garegga's rank failure, where deliberate suicide became the optimal line. Risk of Rain 2, the closest shipped comparable with both a director and stacking items, keys its difficulty to time rather than to what the player owns, for the same reason. Reading pressure also answers the original complaint directly, because a levelled player clearing the back half unpressed is the case that asks for more, and it keeps ADR 0003's floor ladder honest, because a player bleeding weapon levels at the floor is under more pressure rather than less, so the director eases instead of thinning.

Four moments are off limits and the director adds nothing during them (ruled by Mark 2026-09-01, extended by him 2026-09-07). Boss phases, because the authored answer for food during a boss is adds, and Left 4 Dead suspends its wandering horde once a tank fight begins. The sparse last row before each boss (ADR 0051), because it is the held breath, and it is authored as a thin row rather than as a special rule, so a director briefed to fill gaps cannot tell it from any other gap. The Wall, whose crossable-unloaded property is two-sided and fails silently with every test still green. And the swarm set piece that ends the stage's middle section (ADR 0050), which is an authored payout the storm is meant to shred and not a gap to fill. The last two are the set pieces ADR 0042 pins, and each of them names the property it must keep.

The director both raises and lowers. Left 4 Dead's pacing runs build-up, peak, fade and relax, and the relax is what makes the next peak read as one; a director that only ever added could not make a valley, so at high power the authored lulls would become vacations and the dead stretch would simply move. Lowering means the director withholds what it would otherwise have added, never that it removes authored content: the rows stay the floor.

**Absorbed ADR 0006 on 2026-09-18 on Mark's ruling: the authored timeline is the floor this director adds over, and every die draws from its own named seeded stream.** The authored rows are data: a wave is a section-local time, a formation from the named library, a count and a mob type, with the count on the wave and never on the formation, so density tuning never edits a formation. Sections chain by boundary event rather than by an absolute clock, because a shootable boss dies when killed. Every die the game rolls draws from its own named seeded stream, Slay the Spire's pattern, so a pinned seed replays exactly and one system's extra draw cannot walk another system's dice. What 0006 ruled and this record reverses is a fixed authored density with no director at all, which was decided before per-run rosters (ADR 0046) and drop choice (ADR 0034) made one fixed density untunable across builds, and before mobs were food, so added density pays the player in souls and score as it threatens them and staying weak buys nothing but the baseline game. 0006's dont-build on a rate-based spawn faucet retires with it, because the director is that mechanism, bounded here by the floor, the off-limits moments, and the pressure signal. The back half also read as soft to a levelled player on 2026-08-28, but that read predates #79's back-half retune and every 2026-08-31 ruling is still unbuilt, so it is a reason to look rather than a settled finding. The order the work runs in is progression, then the stage floor, then the playing harness, then this director, so the reading that settles it is the harness playing the stage the director sits on rather than a static tuning pass ahead of either.

Shareable-seed comparability is dropped as a goal: a shared seed no longer promises the same stage. Replay is the binding constraint in its place: the director may read only state derived from the seed and the tape's inputs, and its own dice come from its own named seeded streams and never from the spawns stream, so authored placements downstream of a directed fill stay put and a tape alone still rebuilds the run exactly.
