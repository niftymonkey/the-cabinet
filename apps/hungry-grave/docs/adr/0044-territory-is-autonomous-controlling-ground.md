# Territory is autonomous controlling ground

Territory establishes persistent controlling ground on its own clock: it chooses the densest cluster of mobs inside a window anchored ahead of the grave, claims ground there, and the ground pulls, slows and repeatedly damages whatever stands on it for as long as it stays. Levels buy area and only area in this version; control strength and cadence are named later axes, in that order. How the ground arrives is deliberately not ruled: lobbed, rolled forward, burrowed and emerged, erupted from the earth, or a delivery not yet designed can all fulfil the same battlefield job, so delivery is expression, not identity, and the first implementation's choice of one is provisional until discussed and ruled. Ruled by Mark from the 2026-08-27 playtest; recorded 2026-08-27.

This supersedes the swallow-triggered Territory. What the old ruling could not have known: corpses can be banked, so a player-controlled trigger plus a bankable resource turned an autonomous line into an aimed, timed ability, which is a different weapon than the one ruled. Moving to a clock is the bell's own move (ADR 0036), for the bell's own reason: on a clock the line's rate is fixed and tunable instead of riding the collection rate. What stood: the opening beat, the world-frame drift and scroll-owned lifetime, body-overlap touch, frozen-at-birth capture, oldest-evicted housekeeping, and the grasping-hands expression (provisionally, per above). What fell with the trigger: the freshness-to-radius channel, since no swallow feeds the lay; radius passes to the level channel, and the old "levels must not buy radius" rule dies with the confound it guarded against. The one-bite budget also falls: a control zone's identity is many small touches over time in one place, so damage is repeated pulses on a per-patch re-hit delay, held by the patch and keyed by entity id, which keeps the recycled-slot hazard the one-bite ruling actually guarded against.

Two boundary readings ride with the decision. First, against ADR 0035: this targeting is not homing, and 0035 stands unamended with the wisps as the pool's one homing line. Homing as 0035 caps it is a projectile that follows a mob; Territory's ground follows nothing, and its scan sees only a window anchored to the grave, ahead of it and laterally about it, so the player steers Territory with position alone. Positioning stays the skill being exercised; a bounded window is the load-bearing half of this reading, and widening the scan to the whole field would reopen it. Second, against the bell: from level 4 a toll can push mobs out of a patch the ring contains. That is accepted interplay, not a bug and not special-cased in code: both lines serve reclaiming space, a shove is periodic while the pull is continuous, a shove that clears a small patch entirely hands the mob back to the field rather than to a guarantee, and the patch pulse readings are the watcher.

The magnitudes, the cadence value, the window's size and the level ladder are tuning data, not part of this record.
