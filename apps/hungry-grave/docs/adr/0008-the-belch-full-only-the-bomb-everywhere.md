# The belch: full only, and the bomb everywhere

The one button fires only at a full reservoir, never as a partial bomb: one big earned moment, and a partial would dilute the feast set-piece and muddy the belch-timing instruments. Hoarding keeps its cost either way, because charge past full visibly splashes and wastes; this is the documented cure for shmup bomb hoarding.

The belch is the bomb everywhere, boss fights included: it kills every mob on screen, cancels every mob-fire shot on screen, deals a big chunk of boss damage, never pushes a boss, and the pattern resumes immediately, so a wipe buys a breath and a repositioning, not a skip.

The mob wipe was missing from this record until 2026-08-22, and only from this record. The prototype's belch killed every enemy on screen from its first build; the distillation into this ADR kept the bullet cancel and the boss damage and lost the kills by omission. The decision log had argued for the wipe throughout: it calls the loaded belch the run's first full-screen catharsis against the Wall and promises that the corpses of the whole width rain down as the reward, and it debates no-belch Wall lethality, neither of which a bullet cancel can deliver against a curtain of shamblers that damages by contact and fires nothing. Mark confirmed the fix on 2026-08-22.

Belch kills are ordinary kills. They route through the normal damage path, so each one leaves a corpse and counts against the price of the next drop exactly as a storm kill does, and the wipe restarts the swallow economy rather than emptying the field of it. The wipe reaches only mobs that have entered the field: the bomb is scoped to what is on screen, and reaching above the top edge would silently delete authored content the player never saw arrive.
