# A trash minute is a mow, and density is bought with weak bodies

Ruled by Mark 2026-09-09, in his own words: "bullet heaven is mowing and mowing, getting more power and seeing more and harder enemies as you get it; density is bought with weak bodies, never tough ones." A denser minute is paid for by making the ordinary body weaker, never by making it tougher, so more power always means more enemies and harder ones rather than the same enemies wearing more health.

The direction is settled against the genre rather than against a feel. Every shipped survivors-like kills its trash in a few hits from the first minute and carries none of its difficulty on trash health: Vampire Survivors buys each denser minute by making bodies weak again, and Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor's trash sits at its base health even at maximum hazard. The opposite direction, a tough body at mow density, is a field the player's storm cannot clear, which is the state Mark played and called dodging without the power to kill.

The cost is taken eyes-open and it is that trash goes silent. The mow body carries no fire at all; fire lives on the revenant, which is few, tough and all-armed, and on the bosses. That retires the armed share on the mow body, and the armed share was a promise: only a share of a wave is armed and an armed mob looks armed, because picking targets is a skill only if the player can see which mob to pick. Under a mow that skill moves from inside a row to between mob types, the player picking the revenant out of the mow rather than one body out of a file. The alternative, a low armed share left on the mow body, keeps the promise's letter and loses its substance, because one shooter in ten inside a mow is not a target anybody can pick, and it makes the mow body a shooter with worse odds rather than a different thing.

The revenant is the visible armed minority of a heaven minute and it is never the dose of hell. The doses of hell come from bosses, which is the promise ADR 0050 keeps in its own order, so the difference between a trash minute and a boss minute stays a difference in kind rather than a louder drizzle of the same shots. A roster split asked to carry that promise would spend a dose on trash and leave the boss nothing to be.

One consequence, named so it is not mistaken for a regression: the game's first lesson in mob fire can no longer be taught on the mow body, so a lone revenant teaches it instead, which is what ADR 0016 asks of every type as it arrives.

Health, hit counts and the armed share are tuning data and are not this record's. The readings the ruling was taken against live in [../design/mow-ladder-director.md](../design/mow-ladder-director.md).
