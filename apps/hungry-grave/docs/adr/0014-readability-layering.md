# Readability layering

Readability is a day-one rule, not a polish pass. The field draws as one fixed stack, top to bottom: mob fire, the grave's rim, treasure, mob bodies, corpses, the storm, the bell's ring, the belch's eruption, the grave's mouth, the ground. Every player effect sits in one band beneath the food, the storm highest of the three because it is the densest and most informative, and the bell's ring lands opposite the Banshee's tear-rings, which are mob fire: one shape with two owners, separated by layer as well as by palette. Mob fire owns a reserved value band that nothing else on the field may enter, background and mob bodies and food and player effects alike, so the rule is checkable in grayscale rather than by hue (Ikaruga's polarity is a value opposition, which is why it survives colorblindness). No player effect may occlude mob fire, the belch's eruption included, because Hungry Grave ADR 0008 resumes the boss pattern immediately and grants no invulnerability, where Cave bombs and Crimzon Clover's Break both hold invulnerability for the whole blast.

The discriminator between the storm and mob fire is size and shape before brightness: the storm is small, regular and self-similar, mob fire is large, slow and irregular (Cave's grammar, and the reason log entry 4.3 chose the Banshee's rings). Dimming the storm per sprite is a dont-build: additive stacking makes the densest storm the brightest region of the screen, which is where the grave is, and a storm turned down until it is safe stops reading as the reward for swallowing. The requirement is the property, that mob fire wins wherever it overlaps at any density the game can produce, verified at the density the tuned field actually measures, recorded with the tuning revision that produced it and taken as the peak across the autopilot's runs and a human's. The 300-projectile figure this once named was an assumption introduced to run an arithmetic check, never an observation, so the bar is produced by the build rather than asserted ahead of it (raised by Mark 2026-08-19). Additive blending on the storm is a dont-build for the same reason, and it has to be forbidden rather than measured, because neither instrument can see compositing: the palette check reads declared colours and the grayscale screenshot reads one instant. A storm-opacity player setting is deferred (trigger: a tester asks, or the density check fails on a real screen); Halls of Torment ships one.

The three food-and-threat classes stay tellable by silhouette first, brightness second: corpses hold constant size and fade, treasure stays steady-bright and never decays, mob fire stays in its reserved band. The grave is the health bar, since Hungry Grave ADR 0003 has no bar, so its rim reads above the food layer even under a pile while the mouth's interior stays beneath it and food still visibly falls in; a hit announces on at least two channels, one of them not the shrink. Verification is a grayscale screenshot from Hungry Grave ADR 0013's autopilot at target density, with the feel call staying the human's.
