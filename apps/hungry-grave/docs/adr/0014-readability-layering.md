# Readability layering

Readability is a day-one rule, not a polish pass. The field draws as one fixed stack, top to bottom: mob fire, the grave's rim, treasure, mob bodies, corpses, the storm, the grave's mouth, the ground. Mob fire owns a reserved value band that nothing else on the field may enter, background and mob bodies and food and player effects alike, so the rule is checkable in grayscale rather than by hue (Ikaruga's polarity is a value opposition, which is why it survives colorblindness). No player effect may occlude mob fire, the belch's eruption included, because Hungry Grave ADR 0008 resumes the boss pattern immediately and grants no invulnerability, where Cave bombs and Crimzon Clover's Break both hold invulnerability for the whole blast.

The discriminator between the storm and mob fire is size and shape before brightness: the storm is small, regular and self-similar, mob fire is large, slow and irregular (Cave's grammar, and the reason log entry 4.3 chose the Banshee's rings). Dimming the storm per sprite is a dont-build: additive stacking makes the densest storm the brightest region of the screen, which is where the grave is, and a storm turned down until it is safe stops reading as the reward for swallowing. The requirement is the property, that mob fire wins wherever it overlaps at any density the game can produce, verified at the concept doc's 300-projectile target. A storm-opacity player setting is deferred (trigger: a tester asks, or the density check fails on a real screen); Halls of Torment ships one.

The three food-and-threat classes stay tellable by silhouette first, brightness second: corpses hold constant size and fade, treasure stays steady-bright and never decays, mob fire stays in its reserved band. The grave is the health bar, since Hungry Grave ADR 0003 has no bar, so its rim reads above the food layer even under a pile while the mouth's interior stays beneath it and food still visibly falls in; a hit announces on at least two channels, one of them not the shrink. Verification is a grayscale screenshot from Hungry Grave ADR 0013's autopilot at target density, with the feel call staying the human's.
