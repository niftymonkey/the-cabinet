# Readability layering

Mob fire owns a reserved value band that nothing else on the field may enter, checkable in grayscale rather than by hue, because value contrast is the channel that survives colour vision deficiency and WCAG's own contrast formula carries no hue term. **The guarantee runs one way only: anything at or above the floor is mob fire, while mob fire itself is not required to lie entirely inside the band.** The stricter reading, that mob fire must also stay inside the band, was priced and rejected, because it discards the red-means-danger hue signal the field's grammar leans on.

Value is measured as Rec.709 luma on gamma-encoded sRGB, exactly what `filter: grayscale(1)` produces, so the declared-colour test and the grayscale screenshot cannot disagree about what they are looking at. Relative luminance and CIE L* each give a different band and a different list of violators, and neither is permitted. The band's numbers are pinned by the palette test and derived in [../research/readability-value-band.md](../research/readability-value-band.md), not here.

The band's ceiling binds every colour drawn while the field is live, the readouts on top of it and the art that replaces the placeholders included. Colours that only ever draw when the field is not live are declared apart and exempt, and the exemption is held shut by the source scan.
