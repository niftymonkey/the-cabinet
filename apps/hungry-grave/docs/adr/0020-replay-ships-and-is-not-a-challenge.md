# Replay ships and is not a challenge

Replay is a committed player-facing feature and not only a tuning instrument: a player saves a tape they can keep and opens one somebody sent them, watching the run that actually happened as if over the player's shoulder. The commitment is that player-facing replay is part of v1.

A challenge and a replay are different features and neither substitutes for the other. Sharing a seed gives somebody the same dice and their own hands; sharing a tape gives them the run that actually happened, watched rather than played. Folding the two under one glossary term is what once forced the word "replay" onto an avoid-list, and that fold no longer holds. Hungry Grave ADR 0012 keeps the pinned seed as the challenge and the playtest instrument, and this is the other half rather than a variation on it: the stage is authored and a seed varies only columns, drop kind and fire jitter, so a shared seed is a much smaller thing than it sounds.

Verification readback and its boundary are their own record (Hungry Grave ADR 0033), extracted from this file on 2026-08-26; the observation pool is ADR 0021 and the build-time gate is ADR 0022. The reserved player route, the instrument route's scope and the link-sharing deferral live with the player-replay ticket and the game concept's deferred list.
