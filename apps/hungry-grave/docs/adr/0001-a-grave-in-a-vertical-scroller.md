# A grave in a vertical scroller

The game is a Halloween vertical shmup crossed with hole.io: the protagonist is a moving open grave, chosen over a hearse, a war-wagon, and nine other candidates because the swallow economy plus multiplicative growth gives it the highest weapon-saturation potential. The frame is a pure vertical scroller ending in authored stages capped by a final boss; the scroll is the corpse deadline, so the fixed frame is load-bearing.

A free-roam arena is recorded as the deferred survival mode's native frame, and a horizontally panning playfield is a dont-build: shipped shmups use width as repositioning margin rather than roaming room, camera motion taxes bullet readability, and corpse deadlines would expire offscreen.
