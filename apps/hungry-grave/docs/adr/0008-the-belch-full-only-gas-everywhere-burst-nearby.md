# The belch: full only, gas everywhere, burst nearby

The one button fires only at a full reservoir, never as a partial bomb: one big earned moment, and a partial would dilute the feast set-piece and muddy the belch-timing instruments. Charge past full visibly splashes and wastes.

The belch's two effects are separate in scope. The gas smothers every mob-fire shot on the whole field, boss patterns included, and kills nothing. The burst kills the mobs within a local radius of the grave, deals its big chunk of boss damage only when the boss is inside that radius, and never pushes a boss; the pattern resumes immediately. The radius, and the reservoir's refill speed, are tuning data.

Why the split: what made the belch dominant was never its price but its scope, because one press resolved the entire encounter. The 2026-08-31 tapes read the belch at 35% and 46% of all kills with the reservoir full 62% to 79% of the run. A press that clears the air and leaves the mobs walking hands the wave back to the storm. Enter the Gungeon's Blank is the precedent; the survey lives in [../research/panic-button-pricing.md](../research/panic-button-pricing.md).

The bomb-everywhere scope this record carried from 2026-08-22 is superseded by the split. What stood: full-only firing, the dedicated button (ADR 0038), belch kills as ordinary corpse-leaving kills that count against the next drop, the scope limit to mobs that have entered the field, and the free-to-hold, free-to-fire, swallow-refilled cost structure. What the 2026-08-22 ruling could not have known: no instrument existed to read the lean until the tape work landed. One consequence taken eyes-open: the Wall stops being one-press catharsis, and its set-piece property is re-read in play (ADR 0042 restatement pending).

This ruling changes sim behavior; its golden and bot regeneration rides with ADR 0034 (regeneration commit first, ADR 0019).

Decision log entries 9, 4.8, and 1.7.
