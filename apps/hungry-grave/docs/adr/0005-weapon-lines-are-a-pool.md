# Weapon lines are a pool

A weapon line owns its own properties rather than belonging to a category: how it fires (always-on, on each swallow, or on a timer), whether the run starts with it, and how its five levels grow. The earlier split into two floor lines and two burst lines is removed (ruled by Mark 2026-08-19): it was a club with fixed membership, which made a fifth line surgery instead of data, and the timed bell (Hungry Grave ADR 0036) is a line it could not describe at all. Always-on survives as a property a line can have; the categories do not. Vampire Survivors is the precedent for the shape: each weapon carries its own profile, which is how that game ships new weapons without rebalancing everything.

The pool is open by design, and its admission rule is generative rather than restrictive: a new line claims a motion no existing line owns, because the motions must never blur, and it answers the standing constraints every line answers, the mob-fire value band (Hungry Grave ADR 0014) and the homing cap (ADR 0035). The current roster and its level cap live with the game concept and the code, not here.
