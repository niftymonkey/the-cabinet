# Four lines, and homing is quarantined

The arsenal is four lines at five levels each: the soul stream and headstones always on as the floor lines, the wisps and bell firing per swallow as the burst lines, so eating defends the dive that earned it at the moment of commitment.

Homing is the wisps' identity only; nothing else in the arsenal homes, ever, because homing trades power for the game's one skill, positioning, so it stays quarantined behind the swallow verb. Live mobs are never food: contact shrinks the grave and only kills leave corpses, so a big grave can never pass under a wave for free. Grasping hands were cut because their jobs were spoken for, and mouse aiming was declined because position is the whole skill.

The baseline chime and swallow juice fire from the very first swallow regardless of loadout, so RNG drops can never leave the early minutes silent; the bell line upgrades the chime the player already knows into the damage ring.
