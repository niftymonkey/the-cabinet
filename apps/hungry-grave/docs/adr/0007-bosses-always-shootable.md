# Bosses are always shootable

Bosses arrive alone on a phase boundary with chunked health, one authored pattern per chunk, and a short invincible flash at chunk breaks; there are no pure-dodge survival phases anywhere in v1, because the player's storm must always matter and an untouchable boss suspends the fantasy.

Bosses take full bell damage but no pushback, while their adds are pushed normally, so authored patterns never smear (Vampire Survivors precedent: big enemies resist knockback so they cannot bounce off their own threat). Bosses shed food throughout the fight, so the swallow economy never goes dark at the climax.
