# Bosses are always shootable

Bosses arrive alone on a phase boundary with chunked health, one authored pattern per chunk, and a short invincible flash at chunk breaks; there are no pure-dodge survival phases anywhere in v1, because the player's storm must always matter and an untouchable boss suspends the fantasy.

Bosses take full bell damage but no pushback, while their adds are pushed normally, so authored patterns never smear (Vampire Survivors precedent: big enemies resist knockback so they cannot bounce off their own threat). A toll that lands on a boss shows on the boss's body every time, so a hit that moves nothing still reads as a hit; the tell's form and its duration are rendering and tuning rows and not this record's. Bosses shed food throughout the fight, so the swallow economy never goes dark at the climax.

**Amended 2026-09-15, after Mark played the slice H2 build: a toll landing on a boss gets a visible effect on the body even though it is not a pushback.** What stood: the whole of the no-push ruling, full bell damage with zero pushback and the adds pushed normally so authored patterns never smear; the short invincible flash at a chunk break; and the food shed throughout the fight. What it adds: a tell on the boss's body on every toll that lands, beside the phase break's flash and beside the shed pieces the decision log already records, both of which fire on their own beats and neither of which marks an ordinary toll. What it could not have known: that with no push, no damage number and no boss readout anywhere in the game, a toll landing on a boss was invisible in play, so the ruling that protects the authored pattern was silently charging the player the one thing the storm exists to show. The tell's channel, its length and its amplitude are rendering and tuning rows, sized in [../research/boss-hit-tell-and-resisted-push.md](../research/boss-hit-tell-and-resisted-push.md); no figure is ruled here.
