# The field boundary is a readout

The field's boundary is a readout and not scenery: `night` is both the engine's background and the field's ground, so the stroke is the whole statement of where the world ends, and it carries a contrast floor of its own against the ground it sits on. The floor exists because the value band gives mob fire a floor and everything else a ceiling, and asks of nothing else that it be visible at all, which is how the render-structure gate found the boundary at APCA Lc 0.00. The boundary draws on its own layer directly beneath mob fire rather than on the ground, so a body, a corpse or a drop crossing an edge passes under the line; directly beneath mob fire is the highest it can sit without occluding fire, which Hungry Grave ADR 0014 lets nothing do.

The stroke is graded in APCA's fine-detail bracket, which carries no width floor. This supersedes the original grading in the solid non-text bracket, whose 5.5-rendered-pixel floor forced 8 field units of stroke. What changed it: Mark played the field and called the boundary band too heavy, a feel call that outranks a bracket judgement the research record itself declines to call arithmetic. What stood: the floor itself, because the release the original clause named, a ground fill distinct from the surround, was priced and is not available on this palette, where APCA clips to Lc 0.00 for every pair of large fills in the near-black region. The trade is stated because it is a trade and not a free win: a mob-fire core measures Lc 46.3 against the boundary where it used to measure 63.5, and against that, the band in which fire draws over the boundary at all falls from 8 field units to 2. The stroke's width, its colour and the window they sit in are pinned by the palette test and derived in [../research/readability-value-band.md](../research/readability-value-band.md). If the field ever reads as bounded by structure, the floor is released and the reason is written down here.

Extracted from Hungry Grave ADR 0014 on 2026-08-26, decision unchanged; the floor was added by the render-structure gate on 2026-08-20 and regraded by Mark on 2026-08-22.
