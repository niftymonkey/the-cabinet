# The belch: full only, gas everywhere, shove nearby

The one button fires only at a full reservoir, never as a partial bomb: one big earned moment, and a partial would dilute the feast set-piece and muddy the belch-timing instruments. Charge past full visibly splashes and wastes.

The belch's two effects are separate in scope. The gas smothers every mob-fire shot on the whole field, boss patterns included, and kills nothing. The shove throws the mobs within a local radius of the grave away from it in two or three waves, takes health off nothing at all, boss included, and never moves a boss or a set piece's source, because the storm seam answers whether a target may be moved and a boss answers no; the pattern resumes immediately. The radius, the number of waves, their spacing, and the reservoir's refill speed are tuning data.

Why the split: what made the belch dominant was never its price but its scope, because one press resolved the entire encounter. The 2026-08-31 tapes read the belch at 35% and 46% of all kills with the reservoir full 62% to 79% of the run. A press that clears the air and leaves the mobs walking hands the wave back to the storm. Enter the Gungeon's Blank is the precedent; the survey lives in [../research/panic-button-pricing.md](../research/panic-button-pricing.md).

The bomb-everywhere scope this record carried from 2026-08-22 is superseded by the split. What stood: full-only firing, the dedicated button (ADR 0038), belch kills as ordinary corpse-leaving kills that count against the next drop, the scope limit to mobs that have entered the field, and the free-to-hold, free-to-fire, swallow-refilled cost structure. What the 2026-08-22 ruling could not have known: no instrument existed to read the lean until the tape work landed. One consequence taken eyes-open: the Wall stops being one-press catharsis, and its set-piece property is re-read in play (ADR 0042).

**The local kill is superseded by the shove, 2026-09-15, on Mark's ruling that the belch is a big pushback with no damage, two or three shoves.** What stood: full-only firing at a full reservoir, the dedicated button (ADR 0038), the field-wide gas that smothers every mob-fire shot and kills nothing, the scope limit to bodies that have entered the field, charge past full splashing and wasting, and the free-to-hold, free-to-fire, swallow-refilled cost structure. What it replaced: the burst as a kill rule, the chunk of boss damage the burst carried when the boss stood inside its radius, and the flat "nothing is pushed, ever"; the belch-kills-leave-corpses clause of the 2026-08-31 paragraph goes with the kill rather than surviving it, because a belch that kills nothing leaves no corpse of its own. What it could not have known: that removing the kill would leave the belch with nothing a player could name after spending one, which is ticket #124 and Mark's own read of 2026-09-14; and the mow (ADR 0059), under which a local kill radius deletes a handful of one-touch bodies the storm was deleting anyway, so the effect the record priced as dominant had already been eaten by the density around it.

This ruling changes sim behavior; its golden and bot regeneration rides with ADR 0034 (regeneration commit first, ADR 0019).
