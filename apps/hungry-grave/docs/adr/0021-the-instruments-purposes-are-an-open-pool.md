# The instrument's purposes are an open pool

The observation instrument serves an open pool of purposes, by the same idiom as weapon lines and mob types (Hungry Grave ADRs 0005 and 0016): the test for admitting a new kind of evidence is whether it helps the game play better, never whether it appears on a list. Naming the purposes in hand would turn a partial list into a definition and read a third kind of evidence out of scope. The counterweight is the standing constraint on every instrument decision: between two designs, prefer the smallest that preserves trustworthy, repeatable measurement. The pool and the counterweight are the same rule read from either end.

Two consequences bind the seams: evidence is structured and persistable, analysed later without a player interpreting or transcribing telemetry, and the seams are shaped for observation in general, never for the recorder in particular.
