# A set piece names the property it must keep

A set piece names the property it must keep, never the mob types allowed in it (ruled by Mark 2026-08-19, after all three review gates converged on it). Pinning the cast would rebuild the fixed-membership club the pools exist to dismantle (Hungry Grave ADR 0016), at the one place the design cares most, and it would forbid a perfectly good harder curtain built from base trash with the armed share turned up. Leaving it to the author instead fails silently, because a curtain of the type that fires on entry makes the unloaded crossing impossible while every test still passes.

The first instance is the Wall, and its property is that an edge-to-edge curtain built as the belch's target costs more to cross than an unloaded grave has, and that a belch is what opens it. Cannot pass means blocked by cost and never by an impassable body: the grave swallows and passes under, nothing in this game blocks, and a curtain that stopped a body outright would be new physics against the grave's own verb. The cost is carried by the bodies themselves, durable enough that the storm does not thin the curtain into a lane before the grave reaches it, and the opening is geometry rather than a rule: a belch's shove moves the bodies and a gap appears because they moved. No rule anywhere is keyed on which set piece is on the field. Two bot policies carry the property, one crossing without belching and paying a real cost for it, one belching and crossing clean; the unloaded policy is written as a plausible human rather than an optimizer, because a bot proof is an upper bound on perfect play and never a fairness result, so a human crossing stays the anchor. If no authored setting of the wave's count, the shove's waves and their spacing opens a lane the grave can take, the property cannot be met and the instance is cut rather than special-cased; that is Mark's pre-ruling of 2026-09-15 and it belongs to the Wall and not to the rule.

**The unloaded half is superseded, 2026-09-15, on Mark's ruling that the Wall becomes a real wall the grave cannot pass and only a belch opens it.** What stood: that a set piece names the property it must keep and never the cast allowed in it, which is the whole of this record; that the crossing is never free; and the caution that a bot proof is an upper bound on perfect play, so a human crossing stays the anchor. What it replaced: the old two-sided property, "stays crossable unloaded, and is never crossable for free". The unloaded half is withdrawn, and what takes its place is that the belch is the key. What it could not have known: the mow (ADR 0059), because at a one-touch trash body the storm thins the curtain enough for a lane to open and the grave crosses untouched, so the unloaded half was already free and the loaded half went with it, a curtain of silent bodies putting no shots in the air worth a belch; and the belch's own change, which the 2026-08-31 split marked for a re-read in play and which ADR 0008's 2026-09-15 amendment completes, since a belch that kills nothing could not have opened the old curtain at all.
