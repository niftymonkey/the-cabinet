# A hit announces by subtraction

A hit announces by darkening the field rather than by a flash: a brief dim on its own layer, directly beneath mob fire and above the grave's rim, so it is the field between them that darkens and both of the channels that must stay readable are spared. A hit always announces on at least two channels, and one of them is never the shrink. Sparing the rim is not a refinement of the rule, it is the rule: the grave is the health bar (Hungry Grave ADR 0003), so the rim is the second channel, and dimming it would occlude the announcing channel at the exact tick it changes, leaving the player re-reading their own size on a field they have just been knocked around on. The rim holds that job until sound arrives.

Subtraction rather than a flash follows from Hungry Grave ADR 0008: the boss pattern resumes immediately and grants no invulnerability, so the bright flash Cave and Touhou can afford would land here on a field still full of fire, at the one moment the player has to re-read the whole screen from a new position. The shipped precedent is Way of the Passive Fist, which darkens its background so its cues stand out, cited for the game and not for its prescription.

The dim's refractory interval is the grave's post-hit invulnerability window, one number rather than two to keep in sync, because a hit can only land once that window has run out (settled in the sim-core dispatch, 2026-08-20). The floor under that window exists because SC 2.3.1 binds: a full-field luminance swing on this palette is a flash under the criterion, the small-area escape cannot apply to a full-field dim, and SC 2.3.1 invokes Non-Interference, so a game gets no essential-to-functionality exception. The window's value and the worst-case arithmetic live with the tuning number and its test. At the size floor a hit does not shrink, so the rim channel is silent in exactly the state where the second channel matters most, and the ladder events stay three separate events because they are the only second channel left there.
