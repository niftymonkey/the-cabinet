# The first drop seeds, the rest roll uniform

A run's first drop seeds among the weapon lines still at level zero, and every drop after it rolls uniform over all four. One seeded drop still opens a line the birthright does not carry, and everything after it can go deep. The price is paid deliberately: about 5.6% of runs miss a line, against 8.4% under a pure uniform roll and zero under full seeding. The rule stays a pure function of the levels and the drop's own ordinal, so it is testable without running a stage, and a maxed line stays reachable, which keeps ADR 0002's overflow rule live.

This supersedes the first ruling of the same day, seeding every unowned line before rolling uniform. What stood: a seeded opening, and the pure-function shape. What that ruling could not have known: its evidence was simulation, and play showed full seeding sends a run's first three drops to the three lines it does not start with, so no line gains depth and no weapon feels stronger across the minutes that matter most.
