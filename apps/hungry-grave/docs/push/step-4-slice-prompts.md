### The definition, in observable terms

After this slice: a run holds a director that reads one number. It rises on the grave being hit and on the two floor-ladder events, and on nothing else. A body killed beside the grave never moves it, whatever the body was. It holds for five seconds at whatever it reached and then falls at a fixed rate to zero over thirty. It never reads a weapon level, a rung, a line or a roster, and `lineAgnosticPolicies.test.ts` proves that mechanically.

While that number reads low and the section says the director may spend there and the quiet interval has run out and the section's own ceiling is not already met, the director buys one card off the table slice D authored, pays its cost out of the section's purse, and goes quiet for an interval it draws between four and eight seconds. Every die it rolls comes off its own `director` stream and no other stream's cursor moves for it.

`Section` carries a `purse` column: 116 in the Procession, 388 in the Crowd, 0 in the Vigil, and null in the four sections the director may not touch. A spent purse is a section that runs at its authored floor for the rest of its length, and a purse of zero is a section the director looks at and finds nothing in from the first tick, and a reading can tell those two apart. The purse never goes below zero, and an invariant says so on every tick of every build.

Nothing authored is ever removed. A standing wave's arrivals are the floor, so they never count against a section's ceiling, and the ceiling bounds only what the director added over them. The director never places a carrier. It adds nothing during a boss section, under a sparse last wave, over the Wall, or inside the set piece, and every one of those four reads off the section's or the wave's own `directed` cell rather than out of code that names them.

Two runs on one seed with the same inputs rebuild identically, every directed add included, which is #85's acceptance line and ADR 0047's binding constraint.

`pnpm verify` is green, the test-name diff is read against the baseline, `GOLDEN` carries a dated paragraph, and `state.refusals` off this slice's own hand tape is printed in the report.

What a player meets: the space between the authored beats fills in when they are coasting and stays empty when they are being hurt, and a section that has been leaning on them eases off for the rest of its length once it has spent what it had.
