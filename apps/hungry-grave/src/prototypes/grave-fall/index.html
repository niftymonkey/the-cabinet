Knobs

Fall is the natural one. The pull is an acceleration rather than a speed, so a body leans into it, gathers way, and still carries that way when the ground under it ends. Pull strength is about the speed it reaches at the rim.

A body tips over the rim it crosses, keeps the momentum it had, and then falls under the same projection the hole is drawn with. The grave has no bottom, so nothing lands: it shrinks toward the vanishing point, the light dies with depth, and the dark takes it. Drop time is how long that takes.

The grave takes a body whatever its own size. What a body needs over the hole is a share of the most it could have over it, so a body bigger than the opening reaches the same mark by covering the opening, and folds into the mouth on the way in.

Every slider opens centred, so its default has the same room either way. Grave size only moves the grave when you move it; swallowing grows it by the game's own 0.10 units a body and stops at 48, the same top the slider has.

A and B are the older falls, kept beside the new one to compare. Poof is what the game does today.
